# Agent Decker — Player Aid: Turn Sequence

## Key Numbers

- **Line:** 6 cards (conveyor belt — exits right, refills left)
- **Hand:** 4 cards
- **Alarm:** two tokens, 0–50. **At 50 → Game Over**
- **Line fills up? Cards exhausted even after reshuffling the discard? → Game Over ("out of time")**

## Each Turn

1. **Use Cards** — Play cards from your hand to generate **Stealth** / **Fighting**, spend the cost of an obstacle, then choose:
   - **Disarm** — take it to your Play Area (gear, moves, or equip an item with the KEEP icon)
   - **Knock Out** — leave it face down in the line (it won't raise the alarm when it leaves)
   - Cards you use/discard go to the **Play Area**, not your discard.
   - Initially: 1 interaction per turn (rewards may increase this).

2. **Mission Check** — Can you pay the Mission Goal?
   - **Yes** → complete the mission (see Mission Progression below), skip steps 3–4.
   - **No** → continue.

3. **Line Scroll** — Remove the rightmost card to the Obstacles Discard and raise the Alarm by its value (face-down knocked-out cards raise nothing). Slide all remaining line cards one space right.

4. **Card Refresh** — Draw new obstacles into the empty left spots until the line has 6. If the deck runs out, **always** shuffle the Obstacles Discard back into the Obstacles Deck first — Game Over only if there still aren't 6 obstacles in total. Activate any card effects as they appear (they may rearrange the line). Then: discard unused hand cards + Play Area cards, and draw 4 new cards. Next turn starts at step 1.

## Mission Progression (after step 2 says "Yes")

1. **Reward** — Rotate the Mission card to read the top half; place it on the Equip Area (permanent resource or new rule).
2. **Move Forward** — Add the *next* mission's Mission Obstacles to the Obstacles Discard.
3. **New Line** — Shuffle Obstacles Discard + Line + Obstacles Deck into one deck; draw 6 cards (effects activate as they appear) to form the new line.
4. **Reset Alarm** — both tokens back to 0.
5. **Shuffle Deck** — combine Play Area + your Discard + your Deck into a new deck. Equipped items and New Rules stay on the table.
6. **Draw** — 4 cards. Begin the new mission at step 1.

## Resources Cheat Sheet

- **Stealth** / **Fighting** — from playing your cards; spend to pay obstacle costs (any mix)
- **Play Area** — staging zone for used cards; shuffled into your deck between missions
- **Equip Area** — item cards (KEEP) and mission rewards live here across missions
